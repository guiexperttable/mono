Orders – copy, cut and paste

Select cells, Ctrl/⌘+C and paste into Excel or Google Sheets – and back with Ctrl/⌘+V. A single value fills the selected range, a block is tiled. Ctrl/⌘+X marks the cells; they move on the next paste (Esc cancels). Id is read only, prices take “1.234,56” (de-DE) as well.
