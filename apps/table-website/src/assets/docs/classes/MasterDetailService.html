Class MasterDetailService

Master/detail of a table (TableOptionsIf.masterDetail): the expanded master rows, the detail layer and the life cycle of the detail contents. Created by TableScope (tableScope.masterDetailService); use the TableApi (setDetailExpanded, toggleDetail …).

  • Layer: one div ge-table-detail-layer in the host, over the body (full width, fixed columns included, without the scroll bars). It is never cleared by the virtual rendering and does not scroll horizontally.
  • Positions: the heights of the detail rows are extras of the body RowLayout (behind their master row); RenderScope reports the detail rows in view after every render pass (layoutVisible).
  • Life cycle: the content is created when the detail row becomes visible, hidden (not destroyed) when it scrolls out of view, destroyed when it is collapsed, beyond maxRenderedDetails or when the table is destroyed.
  • Events: mouse, keyboard and pointer events from a detail row are ignored by the outer table (isInLayer); the mouse wheel over a detail row scrolls the outer table when nothing in the detail scrolls.
